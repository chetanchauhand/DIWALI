Deepavali, the festival of lights, celebrates the triumph of light over darkness, knowledge over ignorance and good over evil.

The Festival of Lights

Celebrated across India and by communities worldwide, Diwali falls on the new-moon night of the Hindu month of Kartika (October–November). Homes are cleaned and lit with diyas, families gather for Lakshmi Puja, sweets are shared and the sky glows with joy. For many it marks Lord Rama's return to Ayodhya; others honour Goddess Lakshmi, Krishna, or, in Jain and Sikh traditions, their own sacred remembrances.
